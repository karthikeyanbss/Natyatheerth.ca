Guru Sruthi Ganesapandian has been immersed in the world of Bharatanatyam since childhood. Trained under the illustrious Kalakshetra tradition, she completed her Arangetram (debut solo performance) and went on to study under acclaimed masters, deepening her understanding of both the technical and spiritual dimensions of this classical art form.

With over two decades of performance and teaching experience, Sruthi has staged numerous solo and group productions across India and Canada. Her choreographies are noted for their expressive abhinaya, intricate footwork (nritta), and ability to narrate ancient stories with contemporary relevance. She has performed at prestigious cultural festivals, community events, and academic institutions.

In founding Natya Theerth Kalai Koodam in Halifax, Sruthi aimed to create a home for Bharatanatyam in the Maritime provinces of Canada — a space where students of all ages and backgrounds can access this rich heritage. The name "Natya Theerth" (நாட்ய தீர்த்த) means "sacred waters of dance," symbolising the purifying and transformative power of classical dance.

Sruthi's teaching philosophy is rooted in respect for tradition while remaining sensitive to each student's individual journey. She believes that Bharatanatyam is not merely a dance form, but a discipline that cultivates focus, creativity, cultural pride, and physical well-being. Her classes welcome students from age 5 through adulthood, from complete beginners to advanced performers preparing for their Arangetram.
