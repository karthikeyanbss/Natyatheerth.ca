How Adavus Are Taught

Each adavu is introduced with its sollukattu (rhythmic syllables), then taught slowly to ensure correct posture and alignment. Students progressively learn to execute adavus at different speeds (laya) — slow (vilamba), medium (madhya), and fast (druta). The integration of hand gestures (hasthas) with footwork trains the holistic coordination essential to Bharatanatyam.
