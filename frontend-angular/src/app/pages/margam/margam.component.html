The Classical Margam

Margam (மார்கம்) literally means "path" or "way." In Bharatanatyam, it refers to the structured sequence of items that make up a full solo recital. The margam represents a journey — from awakening to full expression to devotional conclusion.

Arangetram – The Debut Recital

When a student completes their training and presents the full margam publicly for the first time, it is called the Arangetram (அரங்கேற்றம்) — "ascending the stage." This milestone is a deeply celebrated moment for the student, their family, and their guru.
