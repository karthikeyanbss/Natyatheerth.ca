About Our Guru

Under the guidance of Guru Sruthi Ganesapandian

SG

Sruthi Ganesapandian

Guru Sruthi Ganesapandian is a dedicated Bharatanatyam dancer and teacher with over two decades of immersion in this classical Indian art form. Trained under renowned masters, she brings the authentic Kalakshetra tradition to Halifax, NS.

Her teaching philosophy embraces both the rigorous classical technique and the spiritual essence of Bharatanatyam, nurturing students to discover their inner artist.
